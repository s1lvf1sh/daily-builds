# Chladni Sand: Value & Future

## Value

A small teaching toy: it shows why nodal lines appear and how degenerate modes mix. A student of waves might enjoy it. It is not a tool people would return to.

## Limitations

- Fixed-edge membrane, not a free-edge plate, so the figures are not those of real Chladni plates.
- Frequencies are relative; there is no sound and no resonance curve.
- Sand piles up on the plate edges (u = 0 there), which is an artefact of the model.
- Grains settle quickly, so the "forming" phase is short.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Free-edge plate via numerical eigen-solve | Real figures; checkable against published ones | L |
| Audio tone at the mode frequency | Sensory link | S |
| Drive at arbitrary frequency with damping | Resonance behaviour | M |
| Save PNG | Shareable art | S |

## Recommended next move

Audio and PNG export are cheap; the free-edge plate is the one that would make it honest physics.
