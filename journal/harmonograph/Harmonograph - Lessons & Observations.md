# Harmonograph: Lessons & Observations

## Surprises and emergent behaviour

- A tiny detune (0.1 to 0.3 percent) turns a closed figure into a slowly rotating weave; the cents read-out explains why.
- The `2ab−a−b` crossing count holds at most phases but sits one lower at 9 of 72 sampled ones, and finer sampling did not change that.

## Engineering lessons

- A random generator must produce values the codec accepts; round-trip tests found it.
- A shell command with a stray `cat > file` and no heredoc blocks for 2 minutes.

## Process lessons

- I wrote another false hand-made claim (sharp versus flat). Derive it.
- Pick the thing with something to do in the first seconds when past verdicts punish niche tools.

## Open questions

- Why does the crossing count dip? Does it stay at 2ab−a−b−1 over a phase interval?
- Will a person find this absorbing or just pretty?
