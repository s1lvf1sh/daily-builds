# Harmonograph: Value & Future

## Value

A toy to watch and tweak; the interval read-out may interest someone who likes the link between ratios and shapes, and the SVG export gives plot-ready line art. It is not a tool people need. Past verdicts suggest a visitor who is not already curious will move on quickly, so my Use and Delight scores are low guesses.

## Limitations

Single pen colour, no sound, no physics beyond independent decaying sines, no touch testing, a fixed 60 s window, the Lissajous count dip is unexplained.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Play the four pitches as tones | The ratio becomes audible | M |
| Pen colour that shifts with time or speed | Visual richness | S |
| Rotary (circular) pendulum | A different family of shapes | M |
| Gallery of saved links | Something to return to | M |

## Recommended next move

Ask a person to try it; if they enjoy it, add sound.
