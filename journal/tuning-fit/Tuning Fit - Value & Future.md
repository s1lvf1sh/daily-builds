# Tuning Fit: Value & Future

## Value
For someone picking a tuning for a microtonal instrument, a scale, or a software synth, it answers "which N, for these ratios" in seconds and in terms they can verify (cents). The Bohlen-Pierce preset shows it is not octave-bound. The numbers are exact; the usefulness is a judgement and untested.

## Limitations
- Audience is narrow. Experts already know the common answers (12, 19, 31, 53) and use tools with richer theory (weighted error, vals, commas).
- Equal weighting of all ratios; no complexity weighting.
- No Scala/Scale export, no keyboard, sine tones only.
- Audio never listened to.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Export .scl for the chosen division | Makes results usable in synths | S |
| Weighted error by ratio complexity | Closer to how theorists rank | M |
| Computer-keyboard player | An actual instrument | M |
| Show consistency and a generator chain | Theory depth | L |

## Recommended next move
Add .scl export; it turns an answer into something someone can load into a synth.
