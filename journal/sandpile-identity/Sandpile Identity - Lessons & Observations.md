# Sandpile Identity: Lessons & Observations

## Surprises and emergent behaviour

The identity is fractal-like and mirror-symmetric although it comes from a two-line formula. A lone 0 sits at the exact centre of odd grids.

## Engineering lessons

- A second, structurally different characterisation (matrix-tree theorem) is a stronger oracle than a second implementation.
- A surviving mutant deserves reasoning, not just another test: mine was equivalent.

## Process lessons

- Everything passed first time, which is itself a reason to run mutations before trusting the suite.
- Different domain (algebra/art) cost no more than another utility.
