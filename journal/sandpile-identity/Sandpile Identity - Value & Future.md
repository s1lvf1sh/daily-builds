# Sandpile Identity: Value & Future

## Value

Mostly curiosity and teaching: it makes an abstract group element visible and testable. A maths student could use it to see the neutral element; nobody else has. Use is low and I rate it so.

## Limitations

- Oracle comparison stops at 25x13.
- Square lattice only, one sink arrangement.
- No explanation beyond a short paragraph; no inverse or group-order display.
- Unused by anyone; clarity untested.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Show group order (spanning-tree count) | A number a student can verify | S |
| Pile inverses and "order of this pile" | Real group exploration | M |
| Other lattices / sink positions | Variety | M |
| PNG export | Shareable art | S |

## Recommended next move

Show the group order and each pile's inverse, since both are exactly checkable the same way.
