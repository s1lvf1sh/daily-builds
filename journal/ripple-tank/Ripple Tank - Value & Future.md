# Ripple Tank: Value & Future

## Value
A toy first: poking water and seeing rings bend around walls is pleasant for a few minutes, and the double-slit and lens scenes show interference and refraction quickly, which a teacher could use. Honest Use is low: it is a demo, not something to return to weekly.

## Limitations
- Variable-speed regions use a form of the wave equation that does not conserve energy across a speed change.
- Frequencies above about 0.06 cycles per step show numerical dispersion.
- Only scalar surface waves: no real water physics (no depth-dependent dispersion, no breaking).
- Not tried by any person; not tested on touch devices or slow phones.
- The slit-width blind spot in the tests.

## Where it could go
| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Divergence-form update for depth changes | Correct energy at interfaces | S |
| Shareable scene links | Send a drawn tank | M |
| Measure tool (wavelength, speed) | Turns it into a lab instrument, raises Use | M |
| Single-slit envelope test | Closes the mutation blind spot | S |
