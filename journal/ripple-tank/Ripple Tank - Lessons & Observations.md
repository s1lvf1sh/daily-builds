# Ripple Tank: Lessons & Observations

## Surprises and emergent behaviour
- The double slit looks right at once: a central bright band with fans either side, from nothing but the local update rule.
- Waves in the lens slow and shorten and the fronts curve toward a focus without any ray code.

## Engineering lessons
- Most of my first failures were test-design errors about the physics: a narrow strip is a waveguide, a sponge in a thin strip eats the wave, a fixed wall inverts the echo. Check what the oracle truly predicts before blaming the engine.
- Numerical dispersion: under about 8 cells per wavelength the shallow-water ratio was 0.574, not 0.6. Resolve at least 14 for checks.
- A mutant that survives (slit width) names a real gap; record it.

## Process lessons
- Screenshots earned their keep: the lens checkerboard was visible there and in no test.
- Do the visual check last: after I changed the lens frequency the screenshot still showed a jagged lens edge, which no test sees.

## Open questions
- Would a person keep playing past a minute, and which scene would they pick?
- Is a measuring tool enough to give it real use?
