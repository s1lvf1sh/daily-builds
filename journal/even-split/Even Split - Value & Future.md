# Even Split: Value & Future

## Value

Useful to a group on a trip or in a shared house who want an answer in a minute without accounts. The typed format and shareable link are convenient. Honestly, existing apps do the core job, so the value is speed and exactness, not novelty.

## Limitations

One currency, single-word names, no percentages, greedy beyond 18 balances, no persistence beyond the link. Unused by anyone but me.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Percent and exact-amount splits | Common real cases | S |
| Multi-word names, quoted | Fewer surprises | S |
| Per-expense breakdown view | Trust in the numbers | S |
| Settlement that prefers fewer, larger payments to named people | Real-world preference | M |

## Recommended next move

Have a real group use it once and note where the syntax trips them up.
