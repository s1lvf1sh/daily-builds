# Lichen Garden: Value & Future

## Value

A small toy to keep open or revisit: a garden that is different tomorrow, the same for everyone on the same date, with a few personal spores and a time slider. As a tool it is worth very little (Use 1.5). As a plaything its value depends on whether the first minute of watching borders move is enough, which I cannot judge.

## Limitations

- The grid saturates in about six months; after that only borders change.
- Spores are local to a browser; there is no shared board.
- UTC days, so the "new day" arrives at the same instant worldwide, not at local midnight.
- The model is a toy, not real lichen biology.
- Untested on other browsers and on a real date rollover.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Rarer, slower events (drought, rain days, a visiting grazer) from the same hash | Lasting variety after saturation | S |
| Make the daily spore matter more (a protected seed, a keystone species) | A reason to plan | S |
| A tiny gallery of shared links | Social loop without a server | M |
| Ask a real person to run it for a week | The evidence this build lacks | S |

## Recommended next move

Get someone to open it on three different days and say what they did. Everything else is guesswork until then.
