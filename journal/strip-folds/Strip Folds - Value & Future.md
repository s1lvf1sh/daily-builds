# Strip Folds: Value & Future

## Value
A small, correct answer to "will this fold flat, and how?" for a strip, with a picture of the result and the dragon-curve link. Honest value: a teaching toy and a curiosity for people who fold paper or like the maths of it. It has no audience beyond that; Use is modest.

## Limitations
- One-dimensional strips only; no 2-D crease patterns.
- Integer lengths from 1 to 99, at most 40 creases.
- Folds are all 180 degrees and the paper has zero thickness.
- Counts past about 10^5 are lower bounds.
- The side view for long strips is tall; it scrolls inside its box.
- Nobody has used it.

## Next steps
1. Fold animation (opening and closing a strip in 3-D), which would raise Delight.
2. A "solve for me" mode: given a target stacking, find the crease string.
3. Export the crease pattern as a printable template.
4. Cross-check against a second public implementation of strip flat-folding.
5. Try the same engine on a ring (closed strip), where parity changes the rules.
