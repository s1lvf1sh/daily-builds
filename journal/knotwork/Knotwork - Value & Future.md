# Knotwork: Value & Future

## Value
An art toy with one real mathematical fact behind it. Useful to someone who wants a quick knotwork pattern as SVG, or who enjoys seeing how wall placement changes the thread count. It is not a need most people have, so Use is low.

## Limitations
- Straight diagonal strands only; no curves.
- Weave direction (which of two mirror weaves) is fixed.
- Mouse or touch only.
- The solver is a heuristic and can fail under heavy symmetry.
- No other person has tried it.

## Where it could go
| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Smooth curved strands | Looks like real knotwork | M |
| Keyboard editing and undo | Accessibility and comfort | S |
| Flip weave button | Both mirror weaves | S |
| Print-ready export | Tile and print sizes | S |
| Knot invariant (e.g. crossing count, writhe) | Teaches something beyond the count | M |

## Recommended next move
Add a flip-weave button and keyboard editing, then ask someone to try it before adding more.
