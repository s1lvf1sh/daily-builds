# Sundial Maker: Value & Future

## Value

For someone who wants to build a garden or classroom sundial, it gives a plate and gnomon that match their latitude, and an honest statement of how far the dial will be from their watch. Use is modest: most people build one dial once.

## Limitations

Horizontal dials only; latitude 5 to 80; Spencer series error up to 0.44 degrees; DST is a tick, not a calendar; the printed result is unverified; polar day/night edge cases are lightly tested.

## Where it could go

| Direction | What it adds | Effort (S/M/L) |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical and equatorial dials | Wall and armillary dials | M |
| Analemma and an EoT-corrected figure-8 | Reads true clock time | M |
| PDF export with page tiling | Large plates | M |
| Real-world test: build one | Proof up to 4 or more | S (needs a person) |

## Recommended next move

Print it and build one at a known place, then compare with a watch on three dates.
