Sparse Ruler

Which marks does a stick need so you can measure every length with it? Fewer marks, same reach.

Click a position on the stick to add or remove a mark. The two ends are always marks. Try to beat the answer.

Lengths you can measure

What "fewest" means here. Two marks a and b measure the length |a−b|. The count includes both ends. With k marks you get at most k(k−1)/2 different lengths, so the count cannot go below that bound. "Proven" means the page tried every smaller count and none works; "not proven" means a heuristic found a ruler of that size, and a smaller one may exist.
