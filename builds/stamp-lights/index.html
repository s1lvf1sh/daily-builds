How it works

The outlined square in the stamp picture is the cell you press. Hover or focus a cell to preview what it will toggle (parts off the board are ignored).

Presses commute and cancel in pairs, so the order never matters and no cell needs pressing twice. The "fewest possible" number is computed exactly by Gaussian elimination over GF(2), then a search over the free choices. Hint recomputes that from the current board, so it stays correct after your own mistakes.

Puzzles are made by scrambling a solved board with random presses, so every one is solvable. The link in the address bar (#level/seed) reproduces a puzzle.
