Tuning Fit

An equal tuning (an "EDO") splits an interval into equal steps. 12 steps per octave is the piano. Which division best matches the pure ratios you care about? Enter the ratios, and it finds the divisions that approximate them better than every smaller one, then lets you hear pure against tempered.
