A sundial tells true sun time. Your watch runs on a zone's mean time, so the two differ by your longitude, daylight saving, and the equation of time (the sun runs fast or slow by up to about 16 minutes through the year). The corrections use the 15th of each month; they depend only on the date, not the year.
