Tile Shifter

Drag a dot on the tile. Its twin edge moves with it, so the shapes still fit together with no gaps.

How can that be true?

A tile whose opposite sides are the same curve, one shifted along from the other, fits against its own copies. A bump you add to one side is a dent on the neighbour's matching side. The page checks that the outline never crosses itself and that its area equals one cell of the grid; if you drag into a bad shape it tells you and keeps the last good one.
