Strip Folds

Write mountain and valley folds along a strip of paper. See whether it folds flat, how many ways, and what the strip looks like when unfolded to right angles.

The strip

Segments are the pieces between creases, so n creases make n+1 segments. Every crease is folded flat (180°). Mountain and valley are as seen from the top face of the strip.

Unfolded to 90°

V turns left, M turns right. Fold a strip in half again and again, open it, and this shape appears.

Verdict

Side view of the flat-folded strip. Each bar is one segment, bottom layer at the bottom; arcs are the creases. Orange to blue runs from the first segment to the last.
