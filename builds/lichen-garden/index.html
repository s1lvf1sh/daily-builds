Lichen Garden

Three lichens grow on one shared calendar. Come back tomorrow and it will have moved on.

How it works

Tap the garden on today to plant a spore (three a day). Drag the slider forward to see the forecast with your spores; back to see the past. A lichen spreads to empty neighbours, faster in summer; winter frost eats the exposed edges; sage beats ochre, ochre beats slate, slate beats sage when three neighbours push.

Everyone shares the same world: the day count starts on 26 Nov 2026 (UTC) and the dice are a fixed hash of day and cell, so the same date gives the same garden everywhere. Only your spores are yours, saved in this browser. A year is 360 days here, midwinter about 21 December. README
