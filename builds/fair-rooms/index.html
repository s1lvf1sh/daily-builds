Fair Rooms

Share a flat? Everyone says what each room is worth to them. You get a room for each person and a price for each room so that nobody would rather have someone else's room at its price.

The flat

Bids are what each room is worth to that person, in the same currency as the rent. Rooms and people are always the same number, so adding a person adds a room.
